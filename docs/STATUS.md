## Known rough edges

* Frame rate unmeasured: every capture ran with Low Power Mode on and
  Roblox open. The last tour ended at 52 fps in the Wastes, which proves
  little either way.
* Two capture-tour cameras are badly placed (the first Wastes shot is black,
  the Ledger Gate shot is inside a building). The game itself is fine there.
* Hair and masks are scaled to the round head rather than cut for it.
